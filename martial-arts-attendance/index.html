A good fit for your studio?

LessonOS is designed for the coach who runs the class and keeps the records.

  • You teach BJJ, karate, taekwondo, kung fu or another small-group class.
  • You want attendance and class-credit balances in one place.
  • You manage the roster on one phone or front-desk tablet.

Each device keeps its own data. There is no live sharing between coaches and no member login app. Pro backups use iCloud on iOS or Google Drive on Android; they do not provide live sync.

Before your first class

Is LessonOS free to try with my class?

Yes. The free plan supports up to 15 students, 3 classes and 1 credit package, including check-in and attendance. Pro adds higher limits and features such as kiosk mode, backups and membership freezes.

Does it work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes, and iPad too. Both platforms support attendance, class credits and membership tracking. Each device stores its own records, and purchases do not transfer between Apple and Google accounts.

Do I need internet at the studio?

Not for day-to-day attendance and local records. Downloading the app, making purchases and using cloud backups require internet.

Can I bring my existing spreadsheet?

Pro includes CSV student import. If you are starting with a small group, you can also add names directly and try one class first.
