QR check-in guide · LessonOS 1.15.0

Arrive. Show your QR. You’re checked in.

A simple arrival routine for your studio. Students bring a saved QR image. The studio’s LessonOS device scans it and records attendance locally.

Your QR goes in front of the studio camera.

The coach selects today’s class and leaves the scanner open nearby. Students don’t need to download LessonOS, create an account or connect to Wi-Fi.

Set up once. Scan each arrival.

SET UP THE MEMBER

Create and share their QR image

Open Students, choose the member and tap the QR icon in Member Detail. Choose Share QR image and send it through your usual messaging app. For children, send it to a parent.

CHOOSE THE CLASS

Open Kiosk, then QR check-in

In Settings, start Kiosk mode. Select today’s class, then choose QR check-in. Allow camera access when asked. Kiosk access follows your existing Pro entitlement.

POSITION THE DEVICE

Keep the camera and screen facing students

Use the device containing your studio’s current records. Place it securely with the front camera facing arrivals, where students can see confirmation. Switch cameras if your setup needs it. Keep the coach nearby.

REVIEW EXCEPTIONS

Leave normal arrivals to the scanner

Eligible scans save attendance and apply the class’s normal credit deduction. A repeat scan does not charge again. If the screen says See your coach, handle that member through the usual manual flow.

Keep your QR handy.

BEFORE CLASS

Save the image your coach sends

Keep it in your photos or a message you can find quickly. A parent can hold a child’s QR image. You don’t need a student account or another app.

WHEN YOU ARRIVE

Show your QR to the studio camera

Open the image, turn up your screen brightness if needed and hold it steadily in front of the camera. Keep the whole QR visible. You are showing your own code, not scanning the studio screen.

CHECK THE RESULT

Wait for your name and Checked in

Once confirmation appears, move your phone away so the next member can scan. Already checked in means your attendance was recorded earlier. For any other message, ask your coach.

IF YOU FORGET YOUR QR

Your coach can still check you in

Ask for a manual check-in or another copy of your QR image. Keep your card private and don’t share it with other members.

Offline means on this device. Attendance is saved in the scanning device’s LessonOS database. A second phone or tablet does not automatically receive those records.

When something needs attention

The camera isn’t reading the image

Show the full QR, avoid reflections and change the distance slowly. Check camera permission and try Switch camera. Name check-in remains available if the camera is unavailable.

The screen says See your coach

No new attendance was recorded by that scan. The member may not be scheduled for this class/date, their membership may need attention, or the coach may already have recorded a different attendance status. Let the coach review it.

A member needs a new QR card

Open their QR membership card and choose Replace card. Share the new image. The previous code stops working in this device’s current database. Restoring an older backup can restore an older card; review cards after restoring.

Can I leave the device completely unattended?

Keep the coach nearby. This is a convenient attendance flow, not an identity check or a secure reception terminal. QR images can be copied, and the current kiosk exit is a hold gesture.
